Comparative Financial & Strategic Analysis

A rigorous 2022–2024 equity evaluation of global beverage titans: The Coca-Cola Company versus PepsiCo, Inc.

Capital Efficiency (ROCE)

PepsiCo sustained superior capital efficiency with ROCE peaking above 18.9% in 2024, leveraging faster working capital turnover and high-velocity snacks (Frito-Lay) to offset margin pressures.

Pricing Power & Moat

Coca-Cola maintained unrivaled gross margins (60.4%) and operating margin (29.2%), validating consumer inelasticity and its high-margin concentrate licensing model.

ESG & Packaging Regulations

Both firms face escalating OPEX risks from EU/UK Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates and single-use plastic taxes, requiring substantial capex reallocation into rPET streams.

Research Abstract

Coca-Cola Co. vs. PepsiCo Inc. (2022–2024)

Executive Objective: This research project provides an empirical comparison between the two preeminent FMCG beverage conglomerates to determine how varying business models (Coca-Cola's asset-light concentrate licensing vs. PepsiCo's integrated food/beverage distribution) managed high-inflation shocks and ESG packaging mandates.

Methodology & Standards:

  • Data audited from Form 10-K filings filed with the SEC (2022–2024).
  • DuPont 3-Tier Decomposition (Operating Margin, Asset Turnover, Financial Leverage).
  • PESTEL macroeconomic scanning with emphasis on sugar taxes and EU Extended Producer Responsibility.

Primary Conclusion: While PepsiCo delivered a consistently higher ROCE (18.9% in 2024) through inventory velocity in snacks, Coca-Cola demonstrated superior moat defense, sustaining a 29.2% operating margin due to brand inelasticity and minimal packaging cost exposure.
